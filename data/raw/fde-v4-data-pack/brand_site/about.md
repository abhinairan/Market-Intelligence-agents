# About Brewbird Coffee Co.
Captured 2026-09-01 from brewbird.example/about

Brewbird Coffee Co. is a Bengaluru coffee company founded in 2021. We roast in small batches and ship across India.

Our ranges:
- **Brewbird Classic**: everyday filter blends and espresso beans.
- **Brewbird Drip**: single-serve drip bags for travel and office.
- **Brewbird Nitro**: nitrogen-infused cold brew in cans. Our fastest-growing line in 2025.
- **Brewbird Origins**: single-estate coffees from Karnataka, launched November 2025.

Brewbird Coffee Co. has no connection with any restaurant, bar or taproom using a similar name.
