# Press note: Saltmarsh launches Nitro
Captured 2026-09-14 from saltmarsh.example/press

Mumbai, 14 September 2026. Saltmarsh Coffee today launched Saltmarsh Nitro, a nitrogen-infused cold brew in a 200ml can. "Cold brew in a can should not be a luxury," said the founder. Pricing starts at Rs 540 for a 6-pack in India. A UAE launch through our Dubai partner follows the same week.
