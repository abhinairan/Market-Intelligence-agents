# FAQ
Captured 2026-09-01 from brewbird.example/faq

**Do you ship free?** Free shipping on orders above Rs 599.
**How should I store Nitro cans?** Refrigerate. Do not freeze. Do not consume a can that is swollen or bulging; write to care@brewbird.example for a replacement.
**Subscriptions?** Monthly subscriptions on Classic and Origins. Cancel any time from your account page.
